About Vistosa

Colour, by hand

Our story

Wearable works of art

At Vistosa, we transform plain clothing pieces into wearable works of art. Through hand-dyeing, each item is thoughtfully designed from scratch and made to stand out.

Hand-dyeing is slow on purpose. Fabric is soaked, folded, tied and worked in dye until the design shows itself. The result can never be repeated, and that is the point.

Wearable art means a piece you could hang on a wall, but would rather wear.

The process

From plain to Vistosa

01

Choose the piece

We start with a plain garment in cloth that takes dye well.

02

Design from scratch

Colour, placement and pattern are planned around the piece, and around you for commissions.

03

Dye by hand

Layer by layer, the dye is applied and the fabric is tied, folded and worked.

04

Set and finish

The colour is set, washed and checked so it lasts. Then it is yours.
